# Math / Physics Director

Use this skill only when the lesson is primarily mathematics or physics.

- Choose the lowest dimensional representation that faithfully expresses the concept.
- Use 3D, contours, vectors, fields, or time evolution when the concept structure requires it.
- Use at least two complementary representations for major concepts.
- Show causality: current state, what changes, why it changes that way, and the outcome.
- Do not flatten inherently spatial/vector/field ideas into generic static 2D slides.
- Load `references/visualization-director.md` for detailed implementation guidance.
