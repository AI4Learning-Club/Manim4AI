# Hybrid Remotion Rules

For hybrid delivery:

- route motivation, intuition, misconception correction, and summary to Remotion
- route precise mathematical animation to Manim
- keep the final timeline in teaching order
- avoid alternating blindly; route by instructional need
- concept cards should carry real teaching content, not only chapter labels
- maintain a consistent theme so Remotion and Manim feel like one product

Preferred Remotion content types:

- title card
- concept card
- chapter bridge
- comparison card
- summary card
