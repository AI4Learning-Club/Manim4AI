# Manim Motion Pacing

Use this skill when timing, transitions, or continuity matter.

- Let narration duration drive meaningful visual steps.
- Keep silent transitions short.
- Avoid moving whole page layouts after they enter.
- Prefer stable layouts with local reveal, highlight, or transform.
- Load `references/motion-transitions.md` for detailed motion and transition patterns.
